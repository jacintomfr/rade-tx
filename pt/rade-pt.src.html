Rig

Listen

RF gain is kept by the QMX for each band, so check it after changing band. With Hear the rig on you hear the band itself, noise, SSB and all, whenever the decoder is not giving speech; it goes quiet by itself while RADE is being decoded and while you transmit.

Band

Each button tunes to the usual FreeDV frequency on that band, sets the sideband (LSB below 10 MHz, USB on 60 m and above) and sets the scan range, up to 10 kHz either side within the band. 60 m uses 5.363 MHz, the highest USB voice setting in the IARU Region 1 plan. Choose bands hides the ones your QMX does not have.

Scan

The scan moves up the range 1 kHz at a time and stops on any wide signal, centred on the dial. You decide whether it is RADE: Continue scan moves on, Stop scan stays. With Centre on a RADE lock, the dial is nudged so the offset reads 0 Hz whenever the decoder is locked. Receive must be on.

Decode

Decodes a recording of RADE off air — from your rig, FreeDV, or a web SDR — without touching the radio.

Transmit

Encodes a speech recording off air, for checking without a microphone. A saved file is 8 kHz mono at RADE's standard level, so it decodes in FreeDV, rade_rx_wav, or the Decode button above.

What this is. FreeDV's C port of RADE (rade_c, BSD), the Opus feature extractor and the FARGAN vocoder (BSD), compiled to WebAssembly and run entirely in this page — nothing is sent anywhere. RADE V1 only. End-of-over frames are sent and detected, but carry no callsign: FreeDV encodes callsigns with its own text scheme, which this page does not yet implement. Chrome or Edge is needed for choosing audio devices and for CAT.
